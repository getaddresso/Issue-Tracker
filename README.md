# Issue Tracker ✨

Welcome to our **Issue Tracker**. You can submit **bugs, feature requests, and general feedback**.

If you have a feature request, check out [our current roadmap](https://docs.addresso.com/Roadmap-175857cd78048162a66fd45110091a2c). It could well be that we're already thinking what you're thinking. Or perhaps you're one step ahead of us!

Every contribution helps **make things better for everyone**, so thanks for being part of the journey! 💜 

## 📝 How to Submit an Issue

1. **Click on "New Issue"**  
2. Fill in the details:  
   - A clear **title**  
   - **Description** of the issue
   - Steps to **reproduce** (if applicable)  
   - **Screenshots** (if helpful)  
3. **Assign a label** (e.g., `bug`, `feature`, `enhancement`) to keep things tidy
4. Hit **"Submit new issue"** 🙏  

## What Happens Next?
- Your issue **syncs automatically** to our **Linear board**
- We'll review it, and ... 
- We’ll reply here.

We might be able to respond simply. Or we may need more info. Or perhaps we'll propose jumping on a call together if you're up for it. After all, we consider our work a conversation.

## Guidelines for a Smooth Flow
✔ Be as clear and concise as possible.  
✔ Check if a similar issue exists before submitting.  
✔ Assign a label (`bug`, `feature`, `enhancement`, etc.) to help us deal with it.  

🤩 Thanks again.
